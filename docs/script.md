# The Rain Shop · 雨铺 · Script
Every spoken line appears as a two-line subtitle: 中文 on top, English below. There are no voices. Sound effects, music and on-screen signs carry the rest.
Times are approximate. Total about 60:00.

---
## ACT ONE · 第一幕 · The Shop

### 1. Plum Rain Morning · 梅雨之晨 (0:00 to 5:00)
- 0:00 Black. Rain sound. Ink spreads across the paper and becomes misty hills. Title card: 雨铺 / The Rain Shop.
- 0:25 The long scroll pan along the canal (as in the style test): bridge, willow, boat, the Rain Shop.
- 1:10 Granny catches a drop. It glows. She pours it into a jar and writes a label with a brush.
  - Label on screen: 梅雨 · 第三日 / Plum rain · day 3
- 1:50 Inside the shop: shelves of glowing jars. Labels: 虎丘雷 / Thunder over Tiger Hill · 初霜 / First frost · 秋雾 / Autumn fog · 笑风 / Giggling breeze. On the altar is one jar with a red seal and no label.
- 2:20 The bride and her mother come in.
  - BRIDE: 婆婆，我的婚礼在六月……能下一点点雪吗？ / Granny, my wedding is in June... could it snow, just a little?
  - GRANNY: 六月雪？贵。 / Snow in June? Expensive.
  - BRIDE'S MOTHER: 她从小就想要。 / She has wanted it since she was little.
  - GRANNY (sighs, takes down a tiny jar): 只够一支舞。别浪费。 / Enough for one dance. Don't waste it.
- 3:10 The rice farmer, muddy, holds out a teacup.
  - FARMER: 一杯毛毛雨，给秧苗。 / A cup of drizzle, for the seedlings.
  - GRANNY: 一杯。不是一桶。 / A cup. Not a bucket.
- 3:40 The poet asks for something sad.
  - POET: 有没有……让人想家的雾？ / Do you have a fog... that makes you miss home?
  - GRANNY: 秋雾。别在屋里开。 / Autumn fog. Don't open it indoors.
- 4:20 Old Ink watches from his post. Granny closes the shutters halfway. She glances at the red jar, then away.

### 2. The Boy on the Boat · 船上的男孩 (5:00 to 10:00)
- 5:00 The ferry comes through the rain. On board is XIAOMAN, 9, with a big backpack and headphones. The FERRYMAN poles.
  - FERRYMAN: 柳湾到了。你外婆的店就在桥边。 / Willow Bend. Your granny's shop is by the bridge.
  - XIAOMAN: 这里有WiFi吗？ / Is there WiFi here?
  - FERRYMAN (laughs): 这里有雨。 / There's rain here.
- 6:00 Granny waits on the steps with an umbrella. They look at each other. Neither smiles.
  - GRANNY: 你妈说你会洗碗。 / Your mother says you can wash dishes.
  - XIAOMAN: ……我妈说你会做饭。 / ...My mother says you can cook.
  - Old Ink snorts.
- 7:00 Inside. Granny taps a board on the wall with three rules in brush ink.
  - BOARD: 一、不准碰罐子。二、不准碰罐子。三、红的那个，永远不准。 / 1. Don't touch the jars. 2. Don't touch the jars. 3. NEVER the red one.
  - XIAOMAN: 第一条和第二条一样。 / Rules one and two are the same.
  - GRANNY: 因为很重要。 / Because it's important.
- 8:00 Supper. Rice, greens, silence. The rain ticks on the roof. Xiaoman's headphones play nothing.
  - XIAOMAN: 罐子里……真的是天气？ / Is there... really weather in the jars?
  - GRANNY: 吃饭。 / Eat.
- 9:00 Xiaoman's small room upstairs. He opens his notebook and draws: a comic of a dragon-grandma breathing out rain clouds. He falls asleep to the sound of rain.

### 3. The Giggling Breeze · 笑风 (10:00 to 14:00)
- 10:00 Morning. Xiaoman tries to help sweep the shop. The broom handle knocks a small jar labelled 笑风 / Giggling breeze.
- 10:30 The jar cracks. A ribbon of pink wind spirals out, giggling (wind-chime sound). Hats fly, the poet's pages swirl down the street, the lotus-seed twins are lifted off their feet, laughing. Old Ink is blown in a full circle around his post.
- 11:40 Granny catches the breeze with a fan and coaxes it back into a new jar. Silence.
  - GRANNY: 你知道一阵笑风要等多久吗？三年！ / Do you know how long it takes to catch a giggling breeze? Three years!
  - XIAOMAN: 我只是想帮忙…… / I was only trying to help...
  - GRANNY: 不用帮。 / I don't need help.
- 12:30 Xiaoman sits alone on the stone bridge and draws. The lotus-seed twins sit down next to him.
  - TWIN A: 你外婆凶吧？ / Your granny's scary, huh?
  - TWIN B: 她以前不这样。我奶奶说的。 / She wasn't always like that. My grandma said so.
  - XIAOMAN: 以前是怎样？ / What was she like before?
  - TWINS (shrug together): 会笑。 / She used to laugh.

### 4. The Red Jar · 红罐子 (14:00 to 18:00)
- 14:00 Night. A candle. Xiaoman wakes and creeps to the stairs.
- 14:40 Below, Granny sits before the altar with the red jar. Her lips move. We hear only rain. Her hand rests on the jar like on a child's head.
- 15:40 The jar gives a tiny pulse of blue light and a deep, far-off rumble. Granny pulls her hand back.
  - GRANNY (whisper): 再等等。还不行。 / Wait a little more. Not yet.
- 16:30 Xiaoman's stair creaks. Granny turns. Darkness. He's gone.
- 17:00 In bed, Xiaoman writes in his notebook: 红罐子里有什么？ / What's in the red jar?
- 17:30 Outside, the rain thins... and stops. The last drop falls from the eave. Dissolve to a hard, bright sun.

## ACT TWO · 第二幕 · The Drought

### 5. No More Rain · 不再下雨 (18:00 to 23:00)
- 18:00 Time-lapse over the hand-scroll town: days pass (a calendar card: 第七天 / Day 7 ... 第三十天 / Day 30 ... 第四十五天 / Day 45). The canal sinks, boats tilt in cracked mud, the lotus droops, the willow yellows.
- 19:30 A queue outside the Rain Shop in the heat. Granny sells jars cheaply, one per family.
  - FARMER: 秧苗快死了。 / The seedlings are dying.
  - GRANNY: 拿两杯。别告诉别人。 / Take two cups. Don't tell anyone.
- 20:30 Xiaoman counts the shelves at night with a lantern. Fewer and fewer jars.
  - XIAOMAN: 还剩四十一个。 / Forty-one left.
  - GRANNY: 够了。 / That's enough.
  - XIAOMAN: 够多久？ / Enough for how long?
  - Granny doesn't answer.
- 21:30 The bride's wedding, in the heat. Granny opens the tiny jar: snow falls for one dance. Everyone stops and looks up. Xiaoman sees Granny almost smile.
- 22:30 The ferryman can't come any more. The canal is mud.
  - FERRYMAN: 船走不了了。 / The boat can't move.

### 6. Boss Qian · 钱老板 (23:00 to 27:00)
- 23:00 A big painted boat with gold trim stops where the water ends. BOSS QIAN steps across planks with a fan and two porters carrying a chest.
  - QIAN: 云婆婆！久仰！我出一箱银子，买你店里所有的天气。 / Granny Yun! An honor! A chest of silver for every weather in your shop.
  - GRANNY: 不卖。 / Not for sale.
  - QIAN: 城里人要太阳拍照，要雪办婚礼——你的罐子能发大财！ / City people want sunshine for photos and snow for weddings. Your jars could make a fortune!
  - GRANNY: 天气不是货。 / Weather isn't merchandise.
- 25:00 Qian strolls along the shelves, stops at the altar.
  - QIAN: 那个红的呢？我出双倍。 / And the red one? I'll pay double.
  - GRANNY (picks up the broom): 出去。 / Out.
- 25:40 Comic chase: Granny sweeps Qian and his porters out of the shop; Old Ink pecks Qian's hat off.
- 26:30 From his boat Qian looks back at the shop with narrowed eyes.
  - QIAN: 越不卖，越值钱。 / The more she refuses, the more it's worth.

### 7. Learning to Catch Weather · 学捉天气 (27:00 to 33:00)
- 27:00 Dawn. Xiaoman is waiting on the steps with the bamboo ladle.
  - XIAOMAN: 教我。 / Teach me.
  - GRANNY: 你会打碎。 / You'll break it.
  - XIAOMAN: 那就教我别打碎。 / Then teach me not to.
  - (a long pause) GRANNY: ……鞋子穿反了。 / ...Your shoes are on the wrong feet.
- 28:00 Lesson one: morning mist on the bridge, with a silk net. He swings too hard; the mist laughs away. Granny moves slowly, and the mist settles into her net like a cat.
  - GRANNY: 天气不喜欢被追。要等它来。 / Weather doesn't like to be chased. Let it come to you.
- 29:30 Lesson two: a sunbeam in a teacup. He holds the cup still for so long his arms shake. A thin gold beam drops in. He gasps; it spills.
- 30:30 Lesson three: the cool of a firefly evening, caught in a paper lantern. He succeeds, a little. Granny nods once.
- 31:30 At the end of the day, alone on the bridge, he breathes slowly and holds out his hands. A tiny cloud, the size of a dumpling, forms above his palms. It stays. It follows him home, bobbing like a puppy.
  - XIAOMAN: 外婆！它跟着我！ / Granny! It's following me!
  - GRANNY: 那就给它起个名字。 / Then give it a name.
  - XIAOMAN: ……包子。 / ...Baozi.
  - Granny laughs out loud, for the first time. The twins, passing by, freeze in shock.

### 8. Sixty Years Ago · 六十年前 (33:00 to 38:00)
- 33:00 Night. A dry thunderstorm with no rain, only flashes. Granny and Xiaoman sit under the eaves. Baozi the cloud sleeps on his knee.
  - XIAOMAN: 红罐子里，是什么？ / What's in the red jar?
  - (a long silence) GRANNY: 一场雨。最大的那一场。 / A rain. The biggest one.
- 33:40 The flashback is shown as a shadow-puppet ink painting on a paper screen, in silhouettes and red. A girl of ten (young Granny). The great flood: the river rises, houses float, a huge storm with a shape inside it, a dragon of rain.
  - GRANNY (v.o. subtitle): 那年我十岁。河水漫过了桥。 / I was ten. The river came over the bridge.
  - GRANNY: 我外婆把最大的罐子交给我，说：只有孩子的手，它才肯进去。 / My grandmother gave me the biggest jar and said: it will only go in for a child's hands.
  - GRANNY: 我捧着罐子，站在桥上。它进去了。 / I held up the jar on the bridge. And it went in.
- 35:40 Back to the present.
  - GRANNY: 从那以后，柳湾的雨，都是路过的小云带来的。 / Since then, Willow Bend's rain has only come from small passing clouds.
  - GRANNY: 今年，小云不来了。 / This year, the small clouds stopped coming.
  - XIAOMAN: 那就打开它！ / Then open it!
  - GRANNY: 打开它，洪水就回来了。我答应过的。 / Open it and the flood comes back. I promised.
- 37:00 Xiaoman lies awake. Baozi floats over him and rains three tiny drops on his forehead. He smiles, then frowns, thinking.

### 9. The Thief · 小偷 (38:00 to 42:00)
- 38:00 Midnight. Shadows on the roof tiles. Qian's porters lower a hook through the skylight. The red jar rises out of the dark.
- 39:00 Old Ink wakes and squawks. Xiaoman wakes. Baozi turns into a thundercloud the size of a melon.
  - XIAOMAN: 外婆！有贼！ / Granny! Thieves!
- 39:30 On the roofs: Qian runs, clutching the jar, with his silk robe flapping.
  - QIAN: 世界上最大的风暴！城里人会排队来看！ / The biggest storm in the world! The city will queue to see it!
- 40:30 Under the stone bridge, Xiaoman grabs the jar. A tug of war. The jar slips and cracks against the stone. A thin thread of blue mist leaks out and curls around both of them. A deep breath sounds from inside.
- 41:30 Qian wrenches the jar free and leaps onto his boat. Granny arrives with a lantern, sees the empty altar, and sits down on the step.

---
## ACT THREE · 第三幕 · Letting Go

### 10. The Chase · 追 (42:00 to 47:00)
- 42:00 Xiaoman runs after Qian, with Baozi and Old Ink, across the dry canal bed, through the hanging laundry and over market stalls. The twins join in on a bicycle.
- 44:00 Qian's boat is stuck in the mud at the town's edge. His porters push and he shouts.
  - QIAN: 推！用力推！ / Push! Push harder!
- 44:40 The crack spreads. The jar breaks in Qian's arms. A flash of blue.
- 45:00 It is not a flood. Coiled on the mud is a DRAGON made of rain: long, thin, see-through, shivering, with eyes like a frightened child's. It tries to rise and falls back.
  - QIAN (backing away): 这……这就是风暴？ / This... this is the storm?
  - XIAOMAN (kneeling slowly, as Granny taught him): 别怕。我不追你。 / Don't be afraid. I won't chase you.
- 46:00 The dragon lets him touch its nose. Xiaoman understands.
  - XIAOMAN: 它不是洪水……它是被关起来了。它要回家。 / It's not a flood... it's been locked up. It wants to go home.
  - XIAOMAN: 它太渴了，飞不起来。 / It's too thirsty to fly.

### 11. Every Jar in the Shop · 所有的罐子 (47:00 to 52:00)
- 47:00 Granny arrives, out of breath, with her lantern. She sees the dragon. The dragon sees her and lowers its head, as if it remembers.
  - GRANNY: 是你……你还这么小。 / It's you... you're still so small.
  - XIAOMAN: 外婆，它需要水。很多很多水。 / Granny, it needs water. Lots and lots of water.
- 48:00 Granny looks back at the town, her shop and her shelves. A long beat.
  - GRANNY: 把罐子都拿来。全部。 / Bring the jars. All of them.
- 48:30 The whole town carries jars in a line across the mud: the farmer, the bride, the poet, the twins, the ferryman. Even Qian, sheepishly, carries two.
- 49:30 Together they open them. Each weather bursts out in its own color: grey-silver drizzle, white June snow, gold sunbeams, violet autumn fog, the pink giggling breeze, and green-black thunder over Tiger Hill. The colors swirl around the dragon and it drinks. It grows. It begins to rise.
- 51:00 It falters, halfway up. Granny takes a tiny jar from inside her jacket, tied with a faded red string.
  - XIAOMAN: 那是什么？ / What's that?
  - GRANNY: 你妈妈出生那天的雨。 / The rain from the day your mother was born.
  - She opens it. A soft warm rain spirals up. The dragon roars and climbs into the clouds.

### 12. The Rain Returns · 雨回来了 (52:00 to 56:00)
- 52:00 Silence. Then one drop lands on Granny's cheek. Then a thousand.
- 52:30 Color floods into the ink town for the first time: green willow, red lanterns, blue water, yellow umbrellas. The canal fills. Boats lift and float. Children dance in the street.
- 53:30 Qian stands soaked, his gold robe dripping, then laughs, a real laugh, and helps the ferryman push off.
  - QIAN: 这个……不卖。 / This one... isn't for sale.
- 54:30 High in the clouds the dragon loops once over Willow Bend. Its tail draws a rainbow.
- 55:00 Granny and Xiaoman stand in the rain without umbrellas.
  - XIAOMAN: 罐子都空了。 / The jars are all empty.
  - GRANNY: 嗯。轻多了。 / Mm. Feels much lighter.

### 13. Autumn · 秋 (56:00 to 60:00)
- 56:00 Autumn. The shop sign has a new line painted beside it: 雨铺 · 婆婆与小满 / The Rain Shop · Granny & Xiaoman. The shelves hold only a few jars, with a new sign: 借天气，记得还 / Borrow weather. Remember to bring it back.
- 57:00 The ferry, on full water. Xiaoman's parents' letter has come; the summer is over. On the steps:
  - GRANNY: 你妈说你会洗碗。 / Your mother says you can wash dishes.
  - XIAOMAN: 我会捉天气。 / I can catch weather.
  - Granny presses a small jar into his hands.
  - GRANNY: 想我的时候，再打开。 / Open it when you miss me.
- 58:00 The ferry pulls away. Granny doesn't wave... then waves with both arms. Old Ink spreads his wings.
- 58:30 The city: a grey high-rise window, rain-streaked. Xiaoman opens the jar. The sound of rain on Willow Bend's tiles, and Baozi floats out, rains three drops on his nose. He smiles.
- 59:00 Credits as hand-scroll ink paintings of the town in color.
